## Brand Story

Dr. Nazmul Alam spent 14 years in Canadian R&D — as Senior Scientist at Deciem (the company behind The Ordinary skincare), as Scientist II at Dalriada Drug Discovery, and as a researcher at the University of Waterloo. He published 17 peer-reviewed papers and holds a Scopus h-index of 15 with 1,400+ citations.

When his family moved to Malaysia, two of his five sons continued to struggle with eczema. Finding that even products marketed as "gentle" and "sensitive" contained SLS, synthetic fragrances, and other known irritants, Dr. Alam decided to formulate what the market was missing — a truly eczema-safe, halal-certified, eco-friendly laundry detergent built on real formulation science, not marketing claims.

CORENAT is that product. Nature at the Core.

### Typography Suggestions

| Use | Font Style | Examples |
|-----|-----------|----------|
| Logo / Brand Name | Bold, geometric sans-serif | Montserrat Bold, Poppins Bold, Inter Bold |
| Tagline | Light weight of the same family | Montserrat Light |
| Body Text | Clean sans-serif | Inter, Open Sans |
| Scientific Content | Monospace for INCI names | JetBrains Mono, Fira Code |

**Rules:**
- Maximum 2 font families across all materials
- Never use script, cursive, or decorative fonts
- Never use Comic Sans, Papyrus, or overly playful fonts
- Headlines are always UPPERCASE for brand name: CORENAT

### Logo Direction

- Clean, bold, geometric
- The word "CORENAT" should be the primary logo element
- Consider incorporating a subtle leaf or molecule motif — but it must feel scientific, not decorative
- Must work in single colour (green on white, white on green, black on white)
- Must be legible at small sizes (product label, social media profile photo)

### Photography & Visual Style

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Clean, well-lit product shots on white/neutral backgrounds | Busy, cluttered backgrounds |
| Close-ups of ingredient labels (for exposé content) | Stock photos of happy families |
| Real lab/formulation settings | Overly styled flat-lays |
| Dr. Alam speaking to camera (authentic, not polished) | Professional model shots |
| Ingredient close-ups (raw materials, surfactant liquids) | Pink/pastel aesthetic |
| Malaysian supermarket aisles (for comparison content) | Generic nature landscapes |

## Key Messaging Framework

### Elevator Pitch (10 seconds)
"CORENAT is Malaysia's first eczema-safe, halal-certified, eco-friendly laundry detergent — formulated by a Ph.D. chemist for his own children."

### Short Description (30 seconds)
"Most 'gentle' laundry detergents still contain SLS, synthetic fragrances, and optical brighteners that leave invisible residue on clothes and trigger eczema flare-ups. CORENAT uses plant-derived APG surfactants — non-ionic, biodegradable, and gentle on compromised skin barriers. Zero fragrance. Zero dyes. Zero compromise. Formulated by Dr. Nazmul Alam, a Ph.D. analytical chemist and father of two boys with eczema. Made in Malaysia. Halal certified."

### The Problem We Solve
Parents of children with eczema and sensitive skin cannot trust "gentle" or "sensitive" labelled detergents because most still contain known irritants. They want a product backed by real science, not marketing claims. No Malaysian brand currently addresses this with genuine formulation expertise, halal certification, and eco-friendly ingredients simultaneously.

### Why CORENAT Is Different
1. **Formulated by a Ph.D. chemist** — not a marketing team
2. **Personal stake** — Dr. Alam's own children have eczema
3. **Ingredient transparency** — every ingredient listed, explained, and justified
4. **Plant-derived surfactant system** — APG (non-ionic), not SLS/SLES (anionic)
5. **Halal certified** — JAKIM, not just "halal-friendly"
6. **Eco-friendly** — biodegradable, no phosphates, recyclable packaging
7. **Made in Malaysia** — local manufacturing, supporting Malaysian industry

## Product Information

### Hero SKU: CORENAT Concentrated Liquid Laundry Detergent

**Sizes:** 500mL and 1L
**Price positioning:** RM29.90-39.90 per 1L (premium)
**Format:** Concentrated liquid in recyclable HDPE bottle
**Scent:** None (fragrance-free is the core feature)
**Suitable for:** Hand-washing and machine-washing
**Target pH:** 7.0-8.5

### Full Ingredient List (INCI)
1. Aqua (Deionized Water) — vehicle
2. Decyl Glucoside — primary surfactant (plant-derived, coconut + corn)
3. Coco Glucoside — co-surfactant (plant-derived)
4. Sodium Citrate — builder/chelant (softens water)
5. Sodium Bicarbonate — alkalinity/deodorizer
6. Glycerin (vegetable) — humectant/hand-conditioning
7. Phenoxyethanol — preservative
8. Sodium Chloride — viscosity modifier
9. Xanthan Gum — thickener

### What's Excluded (and Why — For Content Reference)
| Ingredient | Why It's In Most Detergents | Why CORENAT Excludes It |
|------------|----------------------------|------------------------|
| SLS / SLES | Cheap, effective cleanser | Denatures skin proteins, strips lipids from stratum corneum |
| LABSA | Cheapest surfactant available | Primary irritant, harsh on skin |
| Synthetic fragrance / parfum | Consumer expects "clean smell" | Contains up to 3,000 undisclosed chemicals, common allergen |
| Dyes / colorants | Visual appeal | Unnecessary sensitization risk |
| Optical brighteners | Makes clothes "appear" whiter | UV-reactive chemicals that stay on fabric against skin |
| Enzymes (protease) | Stain removal | Causes irritant/allergic contact dermatitis in sensitized individuals |
| Phosphates / STPP | Water softening | Environmental pollutant, eutrophication |
| MIT / CMIT | Cheap preservative | Known sensitizer, increasingly banned globally |
| Parabens | Preservative | Consumer perception concern, unnecessary for this product type |
