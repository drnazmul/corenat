## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: Ingredient Exposés (40% of content)
**Purpose:** Highest engagement and viral potential. Shows what's really inside "gentle" products.
**Format:** Reel — pick up a product, flip to ingredients, explain what's harmful
**Tone:** Factual, slightly provocative, not fear-mongering
**Examples:**
- "Your 'gentle' detergent contains SLS — here's what that does to skin"
- "This says fragrance-free but has 'parfum' listed — let me explain"
- "I checked every sensitive-skin detergent at AEON. Here's what I found."
- "Optical brighteners — the invisible chemical sitting against your skin right now"
- "Why 'plant-based' on the label doesn't mean what you think"

### Pillar 2: Personal Story (25% of content)
**Purpose:** Emotional connection and trust. People buy from people they relate to.
**Format:** Talking to camera, personal, authentic
**Tone:** Warm, genuine, occasionally vulnerable
**Examples:**
- "Why I left a successful career in Canada to make detergent in Malaysia"
- "Every night we moisturize. Every morning we check for flare-ups."
- "14 years in Canadian R&D labs. Now I'm in my kitchen formulating detergent."
- "What I learned at Deciem about what really goes into products"
- "My son asked me why his skin is different from his brothers'"

### Pillar 3: Science Education (20% of content)
**Purpose:** Establish authority. Position Dr. Alam as THE expert.
**Format:** Whiteboard/paper drawing + voiceover, or talking to camera with props
**Tone:** Teacher-like, clear, simple analogies for complex chemistry
**Examples:**
- "SLS vs APG — 30-second chemistry lesson"
- "How detergent residue causes eczema flare-ups (the 2-5% rule)"
- "What 'pH-balanced' actually means for your skin"
- "Non-ionic vs anionic surfactants — why it matters for eczema"
- "The stratum corneum — your skin's invisible shield (and how detergent breaks it)"

### Pillar 4: Product Development Journey (15% of content)
**Purpose:** Build anticipation. Transparency about the brand-building process.
**Format:** Behind-the-scenes clips, desk/lab work, OEM visits
**Tone:** Entrepreneurial, honest, inviting followers into the process
**Examples:**
- "Day 1 of building CORENAT"
- "Visiting a halal-certified manufacturer in Selangor"
- "Designing the formula — here's what goes in and what stays out"
- "Choosing between 1L and 500mL packaging — help me decide (poll)"
- "The waitlist just hit [X] signups — we're getting closer to manufacturing"

## Reel Creation Framework

Every Reel follows this structure:

### 1. HOOK (First 2 seconds — this determines everything)
The viewer decides to watch or scroll in 2 seconds. The hook must be:
- A bold, surprising, or controversial statement
- A visual that creates curiosity (flipping a bottle, circling an ingredient)
- A question that the viewer needs answered

**Strong hooks:**
- "Your 'gentle' detergent is lying to you."
- "I'm a Ph.D. chemist. Let me show you what's really inside this."
- "Stop buying this if your child has eczema."
- "3,000 chemicals can hide behind this one word."
- "Every dermatologist knows this but detergent companies won't tell you."

**Weak hooks (avoid):**
- "Hi guys, today I want to talk about..." (boring, no curiosity)
- "In this video I'm going to explain..." (too slow)
- "So I've been thinking about detergent..." (no urgency)

### 2. BODY (20-35 seconds — deliver the value)
- Explain the science clearly and simply
- Use analogies: "SLS on your skin is like sandpaper on wet paint"
- Show, don't just tell: hold up products, point at labels, draw diagrams
- Keep one single idea per Reel — don't try to cover everything

### 3. CTA (Last 3-5 seconds — tell them what to do)
- "Follow @corenat.my for more ingredient truth"
- "Comment YES if you'd try a truly eczema-safe detergent"
- "Save this for next time you're shopping for detergent"
- "Share this with a parent whose child has eczema"
- "Link in bio to join the waitlist"

## Filming Guidelines

### Equipment (smartphone only):
- Film with phone's default camera app (better quality than Instagram's camera)
- Portrait mode (9:16 vertical)
- Natural daylight from a window — position yourself facing the window
- Prop phone at eye level against books or a bottle
- Clean, uncluttered background

### On-camera presence:
- Look at the camera lens, not the screen
- Speak naturally — conversational, not scripted-sounding
- It's OK to pause and restart — you'll cut in editing
- Slight nervousness reads as authenticity, not weakness
- Wear a plain, solid-colour shirt (no busy patterns)

### CapCut editing workflow:
1. Import video → trim start/end
2. Cut out pauses, "umm"s, and mistakes
3. Add auto-captions (CapCut → Text → Auto captions)
4. Fix any caption errors manually
5. Add hook text overlay for first 2 seconds (large, bold, centered)
6. Optional: add "CORENAT" watermark in corner
7. Export at 1080p resolution

## Community Engagement Strategy

### Daily habit (15 minutes/day):
1. Search **#eczemamalaysia** and **#sensitiveskinmalaysia**
2. Genuinely comment on 5-10 posts (not promotional — helpful)
3. Example comments:
   - "As a chemist, I can confirm this ingredient is a known irritant. Have you tried switching detergents?"
   - "The residue issue is so real. We noticed a huge difference when we changed our laundry routine."
   - "This is such an important topic. More parents need to be aware of what's in their household products."
4. Follow relevant accounts (eczema communities, dermatologists, natural parenting accounts)
5. Reply to EVERY comment and DM on your own posts — this is critical for algorithm favour

### Never do:
- Spam your brand link in other people's comments
- Copy-paste the same comment everywhere
- Follow/unfollow to gain followers
- Buy followers or engagement
- Argue with people who disagree with you

## Milestone Checkpoints

### After 2 weeks:
- Have 6+ Reels posted
- Profile should be complete with 3 foundation posts + Highlights
- Start seeing which content types get more views
- Adjust hook style based on what's working

### After 4 weeks:
- Have 12+ Reels posted
- First demand-test Reel posted and measured
- 100+ followers minimum
- Clear pattern of which pillar performs best
- Waitlist should have 20+ signups

### After 8 weeks:
- Have 24+ Reels posted
- Second demand-test Reel posted
- Clear demand signal (or pivot decision needed)
- If demand is validated → begin OEM discussions with confidence
- If demand is weak → experiment with Malay subtitles, different hooks, or pivot positioning

### After 12 weeks:
- Content machine is running consistently
- Community is engaged and asking for the product
- Waitlist is substantial enough to justify first manufacturing run
- You know exactly who your customer is from the DMs and comments
- Ready to transition from "building audience" to "launching product"
