## Personal Story (For Content Use)

Dr. Alam is a father of 5 boys aged 7-15. Two of his sons have eczema. The family practices Islamic values, with halal compliance being a core priority. They moved from Canada to Malaysia and found the local market lacking in truly safe household products for sensitive skin.

His frustration with misleading "gentle" and "sensitive" labels — combined with his deep understanding of surfactant chemistry, preservative systems, and regulatory compliance — led him to formulate CORENAT.

**Key narrative points for content:**
- "I spent 14 years analyzing what goes into products. Now I'm building one."
- "At Deciem, I investigated product failures. Now I'm preventing them."
- "I'm not a marketer selling detergent. I'm a chemist who couldn't find what his family needed."
- "My boys' skin is the reason this product exists."
