# CORENAT — Instagram Reel Scripts Library

## How to Use This Document

Each script includes:
- **Hook** — the first 2 seconds (determines if viewer watches or scrolls)
- **Body** — the main content (20-35 seconds)
- **CTA** — call to action (last 3-5 seconds)
- **Stage directions** in [brackets]
- **Caption** — ready to copy-paste when posting
- **Hashtags** — included with each caption

Estimated speaking time is noted. Keep total Reel under 45 seconds.

Studies show that anionic surfactant residue persists in fabric even after multiple rinse cycles, and reactivates upon contact with moisture (sweat). ### Reel SE-02: "SLS vs APG — 30 Seconds"
**Speaking time:** ~30 seconds

[Hold two items or draw two columns on paper]

**HOOK:** "Two surfactants. Same cleaning power. Very different effects on your skin."

**BODY:** "SLS — Sodium Lauryl Sulfate. Anionic. Carries a negative charge that binds to your skin proteins and strips natural oils. Cheap. Harsh."
[Point to other side]
"APG — Alkyl Polyglucoside. Non-ionic. Zero charge. Zero protein interaction. Derived from coconut and corn. Same cleaning power. Zero skin damage."

**CTA:** "CORENAT uses APG. Because the surfactant is the heart of every detergent. Follow @corenat.my"

**Caption:** The surfactant system is the single most important decision in detergent formulation. SLS/SLES are anionic — their negative charge interacts with positively charged skin proteins, disrupting the stratum corneum barrier. APGs (like Decyl Glucoside) are non-ionic — they clean through micelle formation without any charge-based protein interaction. Same cleaning efficacy, fundamentally different skin impact. This is core science, not marketing. #corenat #natureatthecore #SLS #APG #surfactant #chemistry #eczema #sensitiveskin #detergent #sciencefacts #eczemamalaysia #malaysia
