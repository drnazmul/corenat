## BRAND VOICE & TONE

### Voice Attributes:
- **Authoritative but accessible** — use scientific terminology but always explain it simply
- **Direct and factual** — no vague "natural goodness" claims, always specific
- **Personal when appropriate** — the eczema father story is powerful, use it strategically
- **Transparent** — list every ingredient, explain every decision
- **Confident without being arrogant** — let the chemistry speak for itself

### Language Guidelines:
- Content is in **English only**
- Use INCI names alongside common names (e.g., "Decyl Glucoside — a plant-derived surfactant from coconut oil and corn glucose")
- Avoid: "chemical-free" (everything is a chemical), "100% natural" (preservatives are synthetic), "miracle," "revolutionary"
- Use instead: "plant-derived," "formulated for sensitive skin," "science-backed," "zero SLS," "ingredient-transparent"
- Never make medical claims — say "formulated to minimize irritation triggers" not "cures eczema"

### What the brand NEVER does:
- Fear-mongering about chemicals without scientific basis
- Making unsubstantiated health claims
- Attacking competitor brands by name
- Using pink/soft/feminine aesthetics
- Posting generic motivational quotes
- Using emojis excessively

## TECHNICAL PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE (For Accurate Content)

### Formulation:
- Primary surfactant: Decyl Glucoside (APG 0810) — 12%, plant-derived (coconut + corn)
- Co-surfactant: Coco Glucoside — 4%, plant-derived
- Builder: Sodium Citrate — 4% (chelates hard water minerals)
- Alkalinity: Sodium Bicarbonate — 2.5%
- Humectant: Glycerin (vegetable) — 1.5%
- Preservative: Phenoxyethanol — 0.8%
- Thickener: Xanthan Gum — 0.2%
- Viscosity modifier: Sodium Chloride — 1%
- Total active surfactant: ~16%
- pH: 7.0-8.5 (near-neutral)

### What's excluded and why:
- SLS/SLES — denatures skin proteins, strips lipids
- LABSA — primary irritant, cheap but harsh
- Synthetic fragrance — allergic contact dermatitis trigger
- Dyes/optical brighteners — unnecessary sensitization risk
- Enzymes — proteases cause irritant dermatitis in sensitized individuals
- Phosphates — environmental pollutant
- MIT/CMIT — known sensitizer
- Parabens — consumer perception, unnecessary for this product

### Key scientific claims (that can be made accurately):
- "Plant-derived surfactant system" ✓
- "Non-ionic surfactants — zero charge interaction with skin proteins" ✓
- "Biodegradable ingredients" ✓
- "Formulated to minimize detergent residue on fabric" ✓
- "pH-balanced for skin compatibility" ✓
- "Free from SLS, SLES, fragrance, dyes, enzymes, parabens" ✓

### Claims to NEVER make:
- "Cures eczema" ✗
- "Dermatologically tested" ✗ (not yet tested)
- "Hypoallergenic" ✗ (requires specific testing)
- "Chemical-free" ✗ (scientifically inaccurate)
- "100% natural" ✗ (phenoxyethanol is synthetic)
